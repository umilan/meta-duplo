How it works

  1. 1 · Identical files. Byte-identical copies are found by size, then a partial hash, then a full hash. Keep one; move the rest to a holding folder.
  2. 2 · Near duplicates. Same picture with a different name or metadata: re-saved, resized, messenger copies, metadata edits. Bursts are shown separately and never removed automatically.
  3. 3 · Harmonize & rename. One date, place and device per photo, recommended from every copy (including removed ones). You accept, edit or reject; then files get names from your pattern.
  4. 4 · Re-check. With harmonized metadata, matches that were invisible before show up. Sort and filter the list, then decide.
